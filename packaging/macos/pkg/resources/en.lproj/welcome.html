Welcome to Strata Arkyo

This installs the Strata Arkyo project and team management server:

  • the server, running as a system service from startup;
  • the Strata Arkyo menu bar app to control it and open the web interface;
  • the Uninstall Strata Arkyo app.

The apps go into the Applications folder. Your data (database, settings, uploaded files) lives in /Library/Application Support/Arkyo and is kept on upgrade.
